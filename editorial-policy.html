Editorial Policy

How we research, write and correct lessons on Chemistry Dock.

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Students and teachers rely on Chemistry Dock for accurate information. These standards guide everything we publish.

Sources and accuracy

Our explanations follow widely used introductory chemistry curricula. Element data, such as atomic masses, is checked against recognised scientific reference sources and rounded consistently for study purposes.

Educational focus

We focus on concepts, calculations and study skills. We do not publish instructions for experiments or information that could be misused to cause harm. Safety guidance is written in general terms and always defers to qualified teachers.

Writing standards

  • We explain technical vocabulary clearly and consistently.
  • Every guide includes a worked example that learners can check.
  • We note where conventions differ between courses.

Independence

Our content is free and independent. If advertising appears on the website, advertisers have no influence over our lessons.

Corrections

If you spot an error, please tell us through our contact page. We review every report, correct confirmed mistakes promptly and update the page date for significant changes.

Images and tools

Photographs come from Unsplash under the Unsplash License. Interactive tools and diagrams are built by us and tested before publication.
