who we are

About Chemistry Dock

A free, independent learning resource that helps students understand chemistry with clear explanations, interactive tools and plenty of practice.

our story

A safe harbour for chemistry questions

Chemistry Dock started as a set of revision notes passed between students who found textbooks dense and online explanations scattered. We wanted one calm place where the core ideas were explained simply, in the right order, with tools to practise along the way.

The name reflects that goal: a dock is a place to pause, get your bearings and set off again with confidence.

Clear first

We explain ideas in plain language and introduce technical terms only when they help.

Practice built in

Interactive tools let you check your understanding as you learn.

Free for everyone

All lessons and tools are free. The site may be supported by advertising, which never shapes our content.

how we write

Accurate, checked and corrected

Our content follows widely used introductory chemistry curricula and standard reference data. Numbers such as atomic masses are checked against recognised sources and rounded sensibly for study use.

We focus on concepts and calculations. We do not publish instructions for experiments, which should always be carried out with a qualified teacher. Read our Editorial Policy for details.

for teachers

Use us in your classroom

Teachers are welcome to use our explanations, tools and practice questions with their students for non-commercial educational purposes. We are always glad to hear suggestions for new topics.
