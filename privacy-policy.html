Privacy Policy

How Chemistry Dock collects, uses and protects your information.

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Chemistry Dock (“we”, “us”, “our”) operates www.chemistrydock.com, a free educational website about chemistry. This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it and your choices.

Information you provide

If you contact us, we receive your name, email address, chosen topic and message. If you subscribe to Element of the Week, we receive your email address. We use this information only to reply to you or send the newsletter. We do not sell anything and never ask for payment details.

Information collected automatically

We automatically receive technical data such as IP address, browser and device type, operating system, referring page, pages visited and visit times. Our periodic table, molar mass calculator and balancing practice run entirely in your browser; your inputs are not sent to us. See our Cookie Policy for more on cookies.

How we use information

  • To answer questions and corrections.
  • To send the newsletter to subscribers.
  • To understand which lessons are useful and improve them.
  • To keep the website secure and prevent spam.
  • To meet legal obligations.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to measure how the website is used. It uses cookies to collect information such as pages viewed and session length, processed by Google under its own policies; see How Google uses information from sites that use its services. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Advertising

This website may display ads served by Google and other third-party vendors. These vendors use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings or www.aboutads.info/choices.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. We share it only with service providers that help run the website, such as hosting and email delivery, or when required by law.

Retention

Messages are kept only as long as needed to respond. Newsletter addresses are kept until you unsubscribe. Analytics data is retained according to our Google Analytics settings.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the GDPR and CCPA, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information. Contact us to make a request; every newsletter has an unsubscribe link.

Children and students

Our lessons are suitable for students of many ages, but the website is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. Younger students should use the site with a parent or teacher and should not submit personal details. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, please contact us so we can delete it.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including HTTPS encryption. No online system is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date above shows the latest version.
